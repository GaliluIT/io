I'm an artist-researcher from Buenos Aires, working between human-computer interaction, science and technology studies, and participatory design. I study voice: what a voice is, what it is for, and what singing can show us about both. Much of my current work is with people who have had a total laryngectomy, building instruments and methods in which expression and identity count as much as function.

I'm a PhD candidate in the Individualized Interdisciplinary PhD Program at Virginia Tech, advised by Ashley Shew. In 2015 I founded [Intercambios Transorgánicos](intercambios/index.html), an arts-science research program at MUNTREF Centro de Arte y Ciencia (UNTREF) in Buenos Aires that develops interactive interfaces for health, and I still direct it. Before that I worked as an art therapist in transdisciplinary neurorehabilitation. I trained in electronic arts, art therapy and music, and I have composed for and performed with the Linux Laptop Orchestra (L2Ork) many times.
