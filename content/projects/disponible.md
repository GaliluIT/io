*Disponible* (Available) is a performance I took part in during 2019, part of *Sinfonía Big Data* (Big Data Symphony), a multiple stage performance created by Colectivo Dominio Público within the Laboratorio de Periodismo Performático, Revista Anfibia's lab where journalism is told live, on stage. It was performed at Fundación Cazadores in Buenos Aires and at the Festival de Pensamiento Contemporáneo in Rosario.

*Sinfonía Big Data* looks into Big Data as a socio-technological phenomenon, through psychopolitics, surveillance and advertising, in dialogue with their political and technological echoes, internationally and in Argentina. It questions the ways of working that make manipulation possible, and the subjectivities that Big Data helps build, caught in the tension between freedom and control that marks our material and virtual surroundings.
