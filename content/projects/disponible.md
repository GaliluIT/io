*Disponible* (Available) is a performance I took part in during 2019 within the Laboratorio de Periodismo Performático, Revista Anfibia's lab where journalism is told live, on stage. It was performed at Fundación Cazadores in Buenos Aires and at the Festival de Pensamiento Contemporáneo in Rosario.
