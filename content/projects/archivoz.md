Speech technology is usually trained on voices collected under terms the speakers never set. Archivoz works the other way around: a voice corpus whose governance stays with the community that contributes to it.

The technical work so far centers on a corpus of about 24 hours of Argentine Spanish and on how to prepare it. Testing voice activity detection and dereverberation showed that aggressive speech enhancement erases part of what makes each voice recognizable, so the preprocessing pipeline is tuned to keep vocal identity intact.

The project draws on the political economy of technology, in particular Cecilia Rikap's work on intellectual monopoly capitalism and Mariana Mazzucato's work on public value.
