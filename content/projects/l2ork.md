L2Ork, the Linux Laptop Orchestra, was founded at Virginia Tech by Ivica Ico Bukvic. It plays instruments built from laptops, motion controllers and its own software, PD-L2Ork. I have composed for it and performed with it many times.

## Telematic pieces

On December 6, 2022, students and faculty at UNTREF in Buenos Aires and at Virginia Tech performed *Transcontinental Grapevines* together, live, each group in its own country. I co-directed the piece with Ico Bukvic and coordinate the first Argentine group of the PD-L2Ork community, at UNTREF. PD-L2Ork, built on Pure Data, gives each musician one of twelve synthesizer channels, so a piece can be composed by assigning roles and frequency ranges to its harmonic, melodic and rhythmic parts; playing together across continents only takes a good internet connection.

The performers were David Clausell, Uma Futoransky, Joaquín Montecino, Lautaro Sosa and Victoria Villanueva at UNTREF, and Sean Curtin, Tyler David Lee Johnson, Christian Koytchev, Zaire Riddick, Dominic Russo, Jacob Alan Smith and Andrew Wickman at Virginia Tech.

On December 5, 2023, the same Virginia Tech and UNTREF ensemble premiered a new piece, *Territorio Prismático* (Prismatic Territory), at L2Ork's fall concert.

Since 2024 I have directed the L2Ork International Community, a telematic performance network linking Argentina, Mexico and the United States.

## PD-L2Ork hackathons

In 2024 and 2025 I designed and ran the PD-L2Ork hackathons, funded by ICAT–ICTAS grants. The aim was to widen who takes part in creative coding by bringing in students from Historically Black Colleges and Universities and minority-serving institutions in the region.
