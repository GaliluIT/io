L2Ork, the Linux Laptop Orchestra, was founded at Virginia Tech by Ivica Ico Bukvic. It plays instruments built from laptops, motion controllers and its own software, PD-L2Ork. I have composed for it and performed with it many times.

## Telematic pieces

On December 6, 2022, students and faculty at UNTREF in Buenos Aires and at Virginia Tech performed *Transcontinental Grapevines* together, live, each group in its own country. I co-directed the piece with Ico Bukvic and coordinate the first Argentine group of the PD-L2Ork community, at UNTREF. On December 5, 2023, the orchestra premiered *Territorio Prismático*, a new piece composed by the same Virginia Tech and UNTREF ensemble, at L2Ork's fall concert.

Since 2024 I have directed the L2Ork International Community, a telematic performance network linking Argentina, Mexico and the United States.

## PD-L2Ork hackathons

In 2024 and 2025 I designed and ran the PD-L2Ork hackathons, funded by ICAT–ICTAS grants. The aim was to widen who takes part in creative coding by bringing in students from Historically Black Colleges and Universities and minority-serving institutions in the region.
