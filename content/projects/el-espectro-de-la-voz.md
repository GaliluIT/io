*El espectro de la voz* (The Spectrum of the Voice) is an interactive installation that came out of two UNTREF research teams working together: AudioStellar and Intercambios Transorgánicos. We collected the voices of visitors to Tecnópolis because we are interested in the voice as sound material, as raw material and as a problem.

We focus on the traits of identity that show up in our voices: our angry voice is not the same as our bored, worried, excited or relaxed voice, and at the same time each person's voice has complex, changing qualities.
