*Esperando que vuelvan las utopías* (Waiting for the Utopias to Return) is a performance I presented in 2018 at MAR, the contemporary art museum in Mar del Plata.

That year I was also one of the artists selected, with a fellowship, for the museum's *Fronteras Suspendidas* (Suspended Borders) production clinic, which ended with an exhibition at the museum on November 24, 2018.
