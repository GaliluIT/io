*Esperando que vuelvan las utopías* (Waiting for the Utopias to Return) was made for the final exhibition of *Fronteras Suspendidas* (Suspended Borders), the yearly production clinic at MAR, the contemporary art museum of the Province of Buenos Aires. In 2018 I was one of the ten artists selected for it.

The binoculars, the hammer-and-sickle pin and the mid-twentieth-century dress carried the utopias of the title. The chair, built by a member of the lifeguards' union, carried a way of waiting: an active, persistent attention, a body available and on alert. The piece resists the resignation that this system is the only one possible, and asks whether a resistance staged inside the museum can escape the logic of the art system.

The development of the concept, the wall text and the 2019 web version are on the [work's own page](https://galiluit.github.io/esperando-que-vuelvan-las-utopias/).
